# The Eight Copy Formulas (with templates)

Each formula gives you: **mechanism → Durex original → template → non-adult demonstration**.

> Originals are kept in Chinese with an English gloss. The pun is the artifact — translated away, there is nothing left to study. The demonstrations, by contrast, are written as copy that works in English.

---

## Formula 1｜Homophone Substitution

**Mechanism**: swap one syllable of a fixed phrase from the trending topic for a brand or product syllable that sounds the same, while the sentence still reads literally.

**Originals**
- 杜 / 独 (dú, "Durex" / "alone"): 「JOY LEAGUE 今夜不独行」— "no one walks alone tonight" (Justice League)
- 湿 / 十 (shī, "wet" / "ten"): 「万湿如意」(← 万事如意 "may all things go as you wish"), 「杜杜湿乐园」("Durex wet park")
- 日 / 曰 (rì / yuē, "day" / "to say"): 「今晚，日【久】见人心」(← 日久见人心 "time reveals a person's heart")
- 尺 / 只 (chǐ / zhǐ, "ruler" / "only"): 「尺杜允许，放胆去战」(← 尺度允许 "within permitted limits")
- 「Just 比薄」— Justin Bieber × the Xiaomi Mi Note "thinner than" launch
- 「杜 du 饿了」— Baidu Waimai + Ele.me, reading as "Durex is hungry"
- 「李所 ying 得」(← 理所应得 "only right and proper", for Leonardo DiCaprio's Oscar), 「首 ying」, 「梅开五杜」(← 梅开二度 "scoring twice", for Messi)

**Template**: take a `fixed phrase from the topic` → swap one syllable for a brand sound → check the line still reads literally

**English demonstration (an AI course)**
- **"Prompt and circumstance."** (← "Pomp and Circumstance" — the graduation march)
- **"Adapt or AI."** (← "adapt or die")

> ⚠️ The homophone is the lowest barrier to entry and the fastest route to corny. **Use it once per line.** Twice and it turns into a jingle.

## Formula 2｜Number Pun

**Mechanism**: the topic comes with a number → that number means something else in your context.

**Originals**
- 「先来 7 次」— "let's do it 7 times first" (iPhone 7 launch invitation)
- 「This is 7 ?!」
- 「一日等于 24 小时」— "one day equals 24 hours" (Kobe's shirt number going 23 → 24; 日 also reads as a verb)
- 「Happy 1G24 Day」— Programmers' Day, October 24 (1024)
- 「点燃爱火，只需 001 点」— "lighting the fire takes only 001" (the 001 model ↔ 一点点, "just a little")
- Year-in-review format: 「反复听了 69 次」("played it 69 times on repeat"), 「520 小时」("520 hours"), 「有 21 次他都在后面」("21 of those times he was behind you")

**Template**: `the topic's number` + `the same number inside your product` → weld the two together with a single verb

**Non-adult demonstration**
- A course priced at 199 → **"In 199 days you won't remember the $199. You'll remember the course."**
- A "3-hour" course → **"Three hours now. Three years of overtime back."**

## Formula 3｜Sense Hijacking

**Mechanism**: find an **idiom or technical term from a straight-faced setting** whose literal reading also works in your context — then use only the literal reading.

**Originals**
- 「深耕细作」("deep, careful cultivation"), 「心急口快」("hasty of heart, quick of mouth") — the fill-in-the-blank dictionary format
- 「前方确认安全」("the way ahead is confirmed safe") — *The Wandering Earth*
- 「推进之前，安全工作已部署完毕」("before we push forward, the safety work is fully deployed")
- 「博弈的要素：策略与得失」("the elements of a game: strategy, gain and loss") — von Neumann's birthday
- 「撕开真相的面纱」("tear away the veil over the truth") — Sherlock Holmes

**Template**: list 20 pieces of industry jargon → ask of each, "what does this say if read literally?" → keep the one whose literal reading is more vivid than its real meaning

**English demonstration (an AI course)**
- **Hallucination**: "The model isn't hallucinating. You are — about how well you use it."
- **Context window**: "Your question is too long. Its patience is too short."
- **Fine-tuning**: "Don't fine-tune the model. Fine-tune the question."

## Formula 4｜Scene Transplant (let the object speak)

**Mechanism**: pick an object that **is already in the scene but nobody notices**, let it speak in the first person, and have what it says point at the real protagonist.

**Originals**
- 洗衣机说 — "The washing machine says: the intensity of that love beats anything I manage on spin cycle"
- 床头灯说 — "The bedside lamp says: as the night-shift manager, I'd like to discuss my pay"
- 音响说 — "The speaker says: I moonlight as a cheerleader; setting the mood is a support role and I gave it everything"

**Template**: `a supporting object in the scene` + "says" + `one complaint / boast / request` → the reader works out what the protagonist is doing

**This is the best formula available when there is no physical product.**

**English demonstration (an AI course)**
- **The coffee cup says**: he used to refill me five times a night. Now I go cold untouched.
- **The printer says**: nobody has asked me for "one more revision" in three months.
- **The calendar says**: he crossed out the weekly-report row and said he's done filling it in.

## Formula 5｜Character Decomposition ⚠️ Chinese-specific

**Mechanism**: Chinese characters are themselves images. Pull them apart, rearrange them, enlarge a stroke, and the meaning changes.

**Originals**
- 「『日』字有多长，从白天到黑夜罢了」— "How long is the character 日? Just from daybreak to dark." (Summer solstice; the character is stretched vertically. 日 also means "day" and, as a verb, is crude)
- Spring Equinox: 「图解下『春』字」— "let's diagram the character 春 (spring)"
- Start of Summer: 「立，建始也；夏，假也」— quoting the classical gloss, then "today is auspicious for growing, for standing upright"
- 「今亦球精 / 头头事到」— brick-wall graffiti hiding the characters 精 and 头

**Template**: write the theme character → split the radicals → can each component stand as a word on its own? → rebuild a sentence

**Chinese demonstration**
- 智 (wisdom) = 知 (know) + 日 (day) → **「智能的『智』，是每天多知道一点」**— "the 智 in 智能 (intelligence) is knowing a little more each day"

**On English**: there is no real equivalent — the Latin alphabet carries no meaning per glyph. The nearest analogue is splitting a compound or reading an acronym against itself, and it is much weaker; use it sparingly.
- **"AI" read backwards is "IA" — Intelligence Augmented. It isn't replacing you, it's extending you.**

## Formula 6｜Parallelism / Almanac Do-and-Don't

**Mechanism**: the old Chinese almanac format, `宜 XXXX` (auspicious) / `忌 XXXX` (inauspicious), four characters being ideal. Or the comparative `A 不如 B` ("A is not as good as B").

**Originals**
- 宜｜颠三倒四 ("auspicious: topsy-turvy") ｜ 宜｜大动干戈 ("auspicious: taking up arms") ｜ 忌｜三心二意 ("inauspicious: half-hearted") ｜ 忌｜倔强倨傲 ("inauspicious: stubborn and haughty")
- 堵在路上，不如堵在床上 — "better stuck in bed than stuck in traffic"
- 买一百个，不如只买一个 — "better to buy one than to buy a hundred"
- 挤在人群里，不如挤在被窝里 — "better squeezed under the covers than squeezed in a crowd"
- 学走心事半功倍 / 学走肾技高一筹 — "go with the heart and get twice the result; go with the body and get the better technique"

**Template**: `what everyone is currently doing` + "is not as good as" + `what you want them to do` — the two halves structurally identical, one or two words swapped

**English demonstration (an AI course)**
- **A hundred AI newsletters won't beat one workflow you actually shipped.**
- **Auspicious｜building it　　Inauspicious｜bookmarking it**
- **Learn the tool and you'll relearn it next year. Learn the thinking and you won't.**

## Formula 7｜Verse

**Mechanism**: three stanzas of plain scenic description + a final line that lands on a person. **The less the scenery has to do with the subject, the harder the landing hits.**

**Structure**
```
[noun] [verb]
[modifier] [scene]        ← stanza 1: wide shot

[noun] [verb]
[modifier] [scene]        ← stanza 2: mid shot

[noun] [verb]
[modifier] [scene]        ← stanza 3: close shot

[turn]
[the line that lands on "you" / "me"]  ← the only line carrying emotion
```

**Originals**: all 25 poems in full are in section 4 of `corpus.md`.

**English demonstration (an AI course)**
> Wind moves through / the office tower at midnight
> A lamp still on / the seventh untitled document
> The cursor blinks / stopped on line one
>
> You thought what you were missing was inspiration.
> What you were missing was one well-asked question.

> ⚠️ This formula **turns precious faster than any other**. The test: delete the last line — are the first three stanzas still competent description on their own? If they were only ever forcing imagery into place, the whole poem is dead.

## Formula 8｜Inverted Restraint (the highest grade)

**Mechanism**: on the occasion where everyone expects you to do a trick, **don't**. Use the plainest sentence available.

**Originals**
- Ghost Festival: **今晚早回家** — "come home early tonight"
- World AIDS Day: **雨，都是在没带伞的时候下的** — "it always rains when you didn't bring an umbrella"
- Traffic Safety Day: **真正的司机，重视每一次安全** — "a real driver takes every safety measure seriously"
- Zero Discrimination Day: **歧视是人类文明的顽疾** — "discrimination is a chronic disease of human civilization"
- Thanksgiving, to Budweiser: **感谢你让更多人，不用一个人从酒吧回家** — "thank you for making sure fewer people leave the bar alone"

**Template**: recognize the occasion where a joke would be wrong → say one true thing → not one word more

**Why it works**: a brand that has been cheeky all year suddenly going straight builds **character depth** instantly. This is the moment a long-running persona pays out.

**Frequency**: no more than 5 times a year. More than that and you're an inspirational-quotes account.

**Non-adult demonstration**
- During a wave of industry layoffs: **"Tools replace tools. They don't replace people who thought it through."**
- When someone uses AI to fabricate a story that trends: **"It only ever answers in your own image."**
