# Durex Copy Corpus

**English** · [Tiếng Việt](corpus.vi.md)

Collected from 34 public sources (Digitaling, Uisdc, Adquan, Meihua, Zhihu, Woshipm and others) plus 260 original poster images.
Spanning 2011–2023, weighted toward the **golden era, 2011–2017 (Environment Interactive / Jin Pengyuan)**.

> **How to use it**: before writing, skim the matching section for **structure** — do not copy the lines.
> But **do copy the voice** — how short the sentences are, how punctuation is used, verbs vs adjectives, whether the person is "you" or "everyone."
> That layer has already been tabulated in `diction.md`; run through it as well before writing.

> **On language**: the originals are kept in Chinese with an English gloss in quotes. Almost every line here is a pun that only exists in Chinese — replacing it with English would leave nothing to learn from. Read the gloss for meaning; read the Chinese for the mechanism.

## 2. The Seasonal-Marker Series (#杜撰二十四节气#)

A fixed hashtag + Chinese-classical visuals = a serialized asset. **This is the best demonstration of turning content into a recurring column.**

The hashtag itself is a pun: 杜撰 means "to fabricate," and contains 杜, Durex.

- **Start of Spring**: 立春的时候，总会发生点什么…… — "Something always happens at the start of spring…"
- **Rain Water**: 在雨水中，意乱情迷了自己 — "In the rain, you lose your own head"
- **Awakening of Insects**: (decomposing the character 春) 图解下「春」字，教你点书本上学不到的知识 — "let's diagram 'spring' and teach you something the books don't"
- **Pure Brightness**: 宜寻春，你有去山野间寻过春吗？ — "auspicious for seeking spring — have you ever sought spring out in the hills?"
- **Grain Rain**: 谷雨后将迎来更猛烈的湿哒哒，你准备好被滋润了吗？ — "after Grain Rain comes a fiercer wetness — ready to be nourished?"
- **Start of Summer**: 「立，建始也，夏，假也，物至此时皆假大也」…… 今日宜生长，宜直立，宜……你懂的 — the classical gloss, then "today is auspicious for growing, for standing upright, for… you know"
- **Grain Full**: 小满？小小的就能满足？ — "Grain Full? Satisfied with something small?"
- **Summer Solstice**: 夏至狂风暴雨，淋湿了你，凌乱了我 — "the solstice storm soaked you and left me in disarray" / 「日」字有多长，从白天到黑夜罢了 — "how long is the character 日? Just from daybreak to dark"
- **Great Heat**: 大暑的三伏天，热辣此起彼伏，汗湿躁动不安 — "in the dog days, the heat rises and falls in waves, sweat-soaked and restless"
- **Start of Autumn**: 贴秋膘，现在贴在身上的肉，都是当时没发育完的肉 — "on autumn fattening: the flesh you're putting on now is the flesh that never finished developing back then"
- **End of Heat**: 处暑这个无情无义无理取闹的家伙，终结了炎夏，也终结了我的裙底幻想 — "End of Heat, that heartless unreasonable thing, ended the summer and my under-the-skirt fantasies with it"
- **White Dew**: 白露，不白露 — "White Dew: not exposed for nothing" (白露 / 白露 puns on "exposed in vain")
- **Autumn Equinox**: 成熟的秋分时节，并没有让所有人的身体成熟 — "the ripe season of the equinox didn't ripen everybody's body"
- **Frost's Descent**: 霜降的天气，真是碍手碍脚，刚想动手动脚，就开始冻手冻脚 — "Frost's Descent weather really gets in the way — you go to get handsy and your hands freeze"
- **Light Snow**: 今日小雪，请重视头部保暖！ — "Light Snow today — please keep the head warm!"

## 5. Scene Transplant (letting objects speak)

Frequent in 2022–2023. **The best fit for categories with no physical product.**

- **The washing machine says**: the intensity of that love beats anything I manage on spin cycle
- **The bedside lamp says**: work is exhausting, all-nighters constantly. As night-shift manager, I'd like to discuss a raise
- **The speaker says**: I moonlight as a cheerleader. Setting the mood is a support role and I gave it everything

In the same vein:
- However much there is to do, don't do it out of order — you're my number 1 to-do item
- The housework genuinely never ends!
- Lie down, I'll tell you a bedtime story. However the plot lurches, it's always a happy ending
- Why is it that you have no lighter, and yet you set my heart alight?
- I'd like to hear your voice
- Rousing and full-throated — mind the high notes
- There's always something more important. You, for instance

## 6. The Fill-in-the-Blank / Dictionary-Entry Format

The visual is a dictionary page; the copy splits in two: **definition (straight-faced) + example sentence (not straight-faced)**.

- **深耕细作** ("deep and careful cultivation") ｜ Definition: to do something with care and thoroughness. ｜ Example: I love how serious he looks, especially the look of him cultivating me deeply and carefully.
- **心急口快** ("hasty of heart, quick of mouth") ｜ Definition: impatient at heart, unguarded in speech. ｜ Example: I was hasty of heart and quick of mouth tonight, and I hurt her.

## 8. Methodology Notes (from an 8,500-word strategy breakdown)

- **Publishing cadence**: 16,938 posts from 2011–2016, averaging 8 a day. "Sustained high-quality output" is the number-one reason the account was recognized at all
- **Persona**: adjusted from "shut-in male" to **"a slightly wicked gentleman who knows how to live — the experienced-driver type"**. The persona is fixed first; only then can the copy be consistent
- **Short copy, brutal images**: the copy only cues; the image does the cutting
